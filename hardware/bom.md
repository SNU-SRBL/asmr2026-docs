# Bill of Materials (BOM)

The BOM lists the parts for our course robot, a modified bipedal version of [Berkeley Humanoid Lite]({{ '/start-here/berkeley-humanoid-lite/' | relative_url }}).

[Open the BOM (Google Sheets)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iyEf5vpD5X02pnApgsv7T-w1pzX7ER7xuTAFjdJ9EZI/edit?usp=sharing){: .btn .btn-primary .fs-5 }

## Using the BOM

Use this BOM as the **baseline** for your team's robot. You don't need to stick to it exactly: if your team's idea needs other parts or materials, you can extend it.

{: .note }
The sheet is view-only. To keep your own working copy, use **File → Make a copy** in Google Sheets.

## Requesting additional parts

If you need parts or materials that aren't in the BOM, ask the TA, [Seunghoon Kang](mailto:alaska97@snu.ac.kr), to purchase them under the class budget.
