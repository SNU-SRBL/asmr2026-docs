## Humanoid hardware

- **Berkeley Humanoid**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21781) · [project website](https://berkeley-humanoid.com)<br>
  Liao et al., "Berkeley Humanoid: A Research Platform for Learning-based Control", 2024 (arXiv:2407.21781)
- **Berkeley Humanoid Lite**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17249) · [project website](https://lite.berkeley-humanoid.org/) · [documentation](https://berkeley-humanoid-lite.gitbook.io/docs)<br>
  Chi et al., "Demonstrating Berkeley Humanoid Lite: An Open-source, Accessible, and Customizable 3D-printed Humanoid Robot", 2025 (arXiv:2504.17249)
- **MEVITA**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17684) · [project website](https://haraduka.github.io/mevita-hardware/)<br>
  Kawaharazuka et al., "MEVITA: Open-Source Bipedal Robot Assembled from E-Commerce Components via Sheet Metal Welding", 2025 (arXiv:2508.17684)
- **Duke Humanoid**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19795) · [project website](https://generalroboticslab.com/DukeHumanoidv1)<br>
  Xia et al., "The Duke Humanoid: Design and Control For Energy Efficient Bipedal Locomotion Using Passive Dynamics", 2024 (arXiv:2409.19795)
- **MIT Humanoid**: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09025)<br>
  Chignoli et al., "The MIT Humanoid Robot: Design, Motion Planning, and Control For Acrobatic Behaviors", 2021 (arXiv:2104.09025)
- **ROBOTIS OH! GYM!**: [project website](https://www.robotis.com/en/project/ohgym.php)<br>
  ROBOTIS, "OH! GYM! (Open Humanoid GYM)": an open humanoid platform and development environment
- **ROBOTIS open source**: [documentation](https://docs.robotis.com/ko/docs/common/opensource/)<br>
  ROBOTIS's open-source projects, including humanoid robots, simulation models, and hardware documentation
