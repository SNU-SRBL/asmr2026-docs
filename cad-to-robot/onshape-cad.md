## Modifying your robot

Teams are welcome to modify their robot, especially the **body system**.

{: .important }
Keep the **two-leg structure** the same as the baseline model. Modifications should be made on the rest of the robot, such as the body system.

If you modify your robot, make your own CAD model in Onshape, based on the baseline model above. You will use your team's CAD model in the next steps, starting with [Onshape-to-Robot]({{ '/cad-to-robot/onshape-to-robot/' | relative_url }}).
